# KWANGYA visual system

The viewer is a K-pop fan choosing a personal wallpaper. Let the photography dominate; browsing and composing are the main tasks.

- Background #08090e, panel #11141f, text #f3f4fb, secondary #a4a7b7, ice accent #a3bcff.
- Manrope display, DM Sans controls and body; generous typography in the hero, compact metadata on photos.
- Desktop gutters 6.2%, mobile 6%. Four-column gallery to two columns on phones.
- Hero: full-bleed Karina photo blended into dark left space, oversized two-line title, delicate orbit motion, glass phone preview.
- Shape: rectangular portrait photos with 10px corners, small capsule filters, 7px action buttons. Glass belongs to controls floating over imagery.
- Motion: one portrait entrance, restrained orbit and floating preview, small hover zoom. Global pause control and prefers-reduced-motion.
- Studio: protected-focus dialog, preview left, controls right; stacks on mobile. Actual exported pixels drawn by the same canvas used in preview.
- Do not turn the gallery into a shopping cart. Source credits belong in previews and footer.

## Future consistency
Use the existing colors, spacing, typography and components for new features. Add a new visual system only at the user's request. Keep project state in PROJECT_PLAN.md.
